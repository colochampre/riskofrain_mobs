# Melee attack animations and hit timing

Objective: the Stone Golem, Beetle and Lemurian melee attacks deal their damage at the moment of contact in the animation, not when the attack starts, and the animations get a wind-up before the strike.
Branch: `1.20.1-melee-attack-animations` from `1.20.1` (`c900f8e`). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Push, PR and merge are the user's.
Test runner: none in the project. Checks are `./gradlew build`, dedicated servers stopped through rcon (no client classes loaded on the server), and the user's in-game test on Fabric and Forge.

## Specs

User requests (2026-11-14, 2026-11-15): "Más adelante ajustamos además el momento en que se aplica el daño del hit de la animación". On the golem: "la animación del golem es como un aplauso. Junta los brazos. [...] me hubiera gustado que abra los brazos y luego los cierre por delante como lo hace". On the Beetle: "me hubiera gustado que haga un pequeño retroceso con el cuello antes de embestir". On the Lemurian: "da un golpe con el puño, también hacia adelante sin impulso".

- S1: Starting a melee attack starts the animation only. The damage (and the golem's strong knockback) is applied on the server at the impact tick, the tick where the animation reaches contact.
- S2: "Si, que falle si salió del alcance." At the impact tick the hit is checked again with the same reach formula the attack goal uses. It misses (no damage, no knockback) if the target is out of reach, dead or removed. The attack is dropped if the attacker dies before the impact tick.
- S3: Animations are built from explicit phases (wind-up, strike, recovery) over the attack progress, through a small client helper shared by the three models, instead of `Mth.triangleWave`. Phases:
  - Stone Golem: opens both arms to the sides, then closes them fast in front of the body into a clap; contact is when the hands meet.
  - Beetle: pulls its neck and head back, then rams forward with the head; contact is the end of the ram.
  - Lemurian: pulls the punching fist back with a slight torso turn, then throws the punch; contact is full extension. It keeps alternating hands as today.
- S4: "Si hay que alargar la animación incluso, se hace." Attack durations may grow. Each attack cooldown stays at least as long as its animation, so attacks never overlap.
- S5: "El sonido de los ataques incluyen el momento de preparación, asique no se tienen en cuenta. [...] El sonido luego lo ajusto yo." Attack sounds keep playing when the attack starts; their timing is out of scope.
- S6: The impact tick and the duration of each mob are one constant pair in the entity class, read by both the server hit and the client model, so changing the timing moves both.
- S7 (assumption): taking damage during the wind-up does not cancel the attack. The golem and the Beetle stay immobile for the whole animation, as today; the Lemurian keeps moving, as today.
- S8: "limitar el limbswing al caminar de los mobs con animación de caminar. Cuando tienen efectos de velocidad muy altos (como con goat hoof) los movimientos son exagerados. Al parecer aumentan con la velocidad de movimiento." The walk animations of the Stone Golem, Beetle and Lemurian are capped so a speed boost does not make the limbs swing wider or faster than at a normal walk. Assumption: the cap is the value the mob reaches at its base walking speed; above it the mob slides a bit more per step instead of flailing.
- S9: Walk cycles reworked, one mob at a time with an in-game check after each (order: Lemurian, Stone Golem, Beetle). Today every part is `cos(limbSwing * f) * a * amount`, so joints can only move in phase or in opposition and knees bend both ways. The rework adds a shared helper for a quarter-cycle phase offset and for a one-sided bend (`max(0, sin)`), so lower joints fold while the leg swings forward and stay straight while it supports. Per mob: Lemurian (digitigrade, like a deer's hind leg): hip, knee, hock and foot on one frequency, the lower segments folding together to lift the foot; fixes today's mismatched right leg frequency (0.5 against 1) and foot amplitudes (2.0 against 1.6), and decides on the commented-out third segment. Stone Golem: knees with their own phase and one-sided bend; forearm and hand frequency 0.75 moved to a harmonic of the legs (0.5) so arms stay in step; torso turn amplitude (1.6 rad) reviewed. Beetle: keeps its symmetric bound (front pair together, hind pair together, opposite); front legs fold while swinging forward; the 1.25 against 0.65 front arm amplitudes checked against the rig.
- S10: "agregamos a la lista de caminatas a la torreta, que tiene cuatro patas para 'clavarse al suelo' cuando dispara, pero quisiera que tenga la animación de caminata, por ahora solo cuando el jugador lo desplaza con una rienda leash". The Gunner Turret gets a four-leg walk cycle that plays only while it is leashed and moving; otherwise its legs keep today's planted pose.
- S11: "el sonido de los pasos sigue el formato anterior. El mob da un par de pasos, pero con 8 Goat hoof reproduce muchos más pasos." Vanilla plays a step every ~1.67 blocks walked (`Entity.move`, distance based), so after the T7 cap the sound outruns the legs. Step sounds of the Stone Golem, Beetle and Lemurian follow the walk clock instead: vanilla server steps are turned off for them and each client plays the step locally when the clock crosses a foot contact. User accepted that this also changes the cadence at normal speed (the Lemurian goes from one step every ~11 ticks to one per footfall, ~5 ticks): "Me parece bien". Foot contacts are defined per mob next to the walk cycle, so T8-T10 move them with the rework.
- S12: "Me parece bien lo de la tolerancia [...] Dale, usá esos márgenes". The impact-tick reach check (S2) allows an extra margin over the reach that starts the attack: the distance a walking player (about 0.216 blocks per tick) covers after a 5-tick reaction until the impact tick. Lemurian 0.65, Beetle 1.1, Stone Golem 1.75 blocks. Walking away still gets hit; dodging needs an early sprint.
- S13: "Animación de pasos laterales para el strafing del lemurian (cuando esta haciendo strafing, la animacion de caminar sigue siendo hacia adelante, a pesar de que se desplaza hacia los costados)". The Lemurian blends a sidestep cycle by how much of its movement is sideways relative to its body (pure strafe: sidestep only; diagonal: a mix), on the same walk system (vanilla cadence, capped amount, steps at foot contacts).
